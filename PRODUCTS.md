# Household & Small Business

All 12 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## The Apprentice

265 tests

A back office for a tradesperson: turns what they know into a sellable course, opens a booking diary, and raises, sends and chases invoices until they are paid.

## Devadex Plain Letter

181 tests

Drop in a confusing official letter; it rewrites it in plain language, says what the letter actually wants, and drafts a reply.

## Devadex Continuity Keeper

123 tests

Reads a story bible, game lore or design document set and reports where files contradict each other. A character who dies then speaks, a town coastal on one page and landlocked on another.

## Devadex Time Machine for Folders

92 tests

Version history for any folder, described in plain English instead of technical differences. “You deleted the pricing section on Tuesday.”

## Devadex Audiobook QC Suite

45 tests

Catches the mistakes in an audiobook chapter before it ships and checks the file against a retailer's technical rules.

## Devadex Subscription Forensics

37 tests

Audits the recurring payments a household is quietly making and shows what is actually being charged.

## Devadex Refund Recovery

35 tests

Reads twelve months of bank statements line by line to catch price rises, forgotten memberships and duplicate charges.

## Devadex Move Manifest

28 tests

Documents a household move so that when movers damage or lose something, the claim payout matches what was actually lost.

## Devadex Offline E-Signature

27 tests

Sign a PDF quote or contract with a legally valid electronic signature on your own computer. No account, no monthly fee, no document limit, no internet.

## Devadex Document Suite

58 files, 56 tests

Document creation and signing bundle. The most substantial application in the lot and the best tested of the three.

## Devadex Batch

27 files, 10 tests

A home-lab discovery engine: define an experiment, sourdough, espresso, hydroponics, brewing, and it proposes the next run to try and why, learning from logged results.

## Devadex Fork

27 files, 7 tests

A what-if decision simulator: model a real decision, new job, buy versus rent, relocate, start a business, and see each path play out over years in money and in day-to-day life.
